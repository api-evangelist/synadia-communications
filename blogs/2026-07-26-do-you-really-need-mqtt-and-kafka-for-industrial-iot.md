The standard industrial IoT stack bridges an MQTT broker into Kafka, and the cost is the glue: two distributed systems to run, two security and monitoring models, and a bridge that is itself a failure point in the middle of your most important data path. Consolidating onto a second proprietary broker trades an integration problem for a procurement problem. Here is the third option.
